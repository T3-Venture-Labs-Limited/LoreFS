# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. We want LoreFS to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and send changes without being talked down to.

- Assume good faith. Say what you mean plainly and politely.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Keep private things private: do not post other people's details or any secrets.
- Maintainers may remove comments, close threads and block people who do not follow this.

To report a conduct problem privately, use [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/T3-Venture-Labs-Limited/LoreFS/security/advisories/new) and say it is about conduct.

This text is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
